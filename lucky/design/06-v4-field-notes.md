# Field Notes: v4 (the suite-forced revision)

Observations collected while building lucky4 against the six-experiment
suite (comparisons/05). As always: what surprised, what resisted, what
opened.

## 1. The suite disagreed with itself, and that was the best data

`paradox_suite.py` announces the Liar "contained" at the lattice center;
`critique_suite.py` shows the revenge version has no fixed point at all.
Both are right about their own construction — the difference is a damping
constant one of them never mentions. This is the entire Lucky thesis in
miniature: the disagreement between two honest instruments is not noise to
be averaged away; it is a pointer at an unregistered choice. v4's
`iterate()` exists because the two files argued.

## 2. An orbit as a return value changes what "answer" means

Once `Cycle` existed, the revenge sentence stopped being a pathology and
became a well-posed question with a non-point answer. The kernel did not
resolve the paradox; it widened the type of thing an answer can be until
the paradox fit. This is the same move as v0's blur (widening the type of
verdict) and v2's Gap-with-notes (widening the type of failure). A pattern
worth naming for the design record: **Lucky evolves by widening return
types, never by strengthening claims.**

## 3. Frames are not correlation, and the difference took a while to see

The first draft tried to express observer-indexing through the v2
SourceGraph (put Alice and Bob in a correlation group). It was wrong in a
precise way: correlation says "these voices overcount when combined";
frames say "these voices may not be combinable at all, and if you combine
them the contradiction you get is YOURS." Correlation discounts a merge;
frames indict one. The ledger entry for a cross-frame fusion charges the
fuser, not the sources — that asymmetry is the new structure.

## 4. The pun and the superobserver are the same shape, and the kernel now says so

`fuse_frames` produces identical marks for the black-hole fusion and the
context-stripped homonym. Two domains that share no vocabulary produce
byte-identical receipts. When one structure covers cases this far apart
without stretching, that is usually evidence it was already latent in the
design (the trail was always the place where acts got receipts; fusion is
just an act that finally got its own).

## 5. Mass wanted to be more than a count, and was refused

Every draft of `mass()` tried to grow: weight the count by source
reliability, discount by correlation group, normalize across combination
modes. Each growth imported a model we do not have and buried it in a
number. The registered version is deliberately poor — a bare count — with
its poverty stated in the choice record. The suite's finding was that
volume was *invisible*, not that volume was *unmeasured*. Making it
visible is the honest fraction of the fix.

## 6. The encoding slot exposes v4 itself

`testify4` marks undeclared encodings. But `framed()` attaches frames with
no declaration of how the frame was chosen, and `forbid_fusion` accepts a
`why` string it cannot check. The encoding critique regresses: every place
the world enters the kernel (weights, frames, fusion bans, damping values)
is an unvalidatable intake. v4 registers the weight intake; the others are
now visibly the same kind of hole. Left open, recorded here: **a uniform
intake discipline — everything entering from the world carries a stated
origin — is the natural v5 corridor.**

## 7. What the suite could not force

Nothing in the six files touches necessity-driven genesis (our pressure
meters and vocabulary growth), the residue ledger, or dialogical adoption
— the suite critiques a logic, and those parts of Lucky are not a logic.
The strongest external test yet still only reached the evidential core.
Either the rest is untestable from outside (worrying) or it needs a
different kind of external instrument (an interacting critic, not a
batch of encodings). Registered as a horizon question, not a comfort.
