# Field Notes from the v3 Absorption

Three structures taken from the Etau/LPA2v tradition (per the comparative
audit in comparisons/04), one deliberately refused, all with birth records
in the CHOICES register. Observations from the first run:

## 1. The two sensitivity axes immediately disagreed — and that was the point

Demo 2's "settled but impure" case: verdict **true** with flip@0.02 and
apex@0.22. The absorbed apex axis and our native edge axis told different
stories about the same verdict — barely inside the region (one nudge from
blur) AND far from ideal support (carrying real contrary evidence). The
near-ideal case read flip@0.39, apex@0.03: deeply settled, nearly pure.

v1 would have printed "true" for both, and even v1's flip distance alone
would have caught only half the difference. A reader now gets coordinates
(which region, how deep) and bearings (how far from ideal, leaning which
way). The registered price: two numbers where readers want one. Confirmed
worth paying on first contact.

## 2. Tendency vocabulary turned out richer than their glyphs

We absorbed the *idea* of the 12-region interior, not the labels. Their
Qt->conflict glyph became "toward-true/toward-conflict (certainty-led)" —
and the third component (which dimension is driving: certainty-led /
knowledge-led / balanced) is not in their label set at all; it fell out of
comparing |Gc| against |Gct| while implementing. Absorption produced a
small mutation that exceeds the absorbed structure. This is how borrowing
should work: the birth record names the source AND the difference.

## 3. The tilted-carving tag closed a hole we didn't know v1 had

Demo 3: the same evidence reads "true" under a neutral carving and
"blur ~toward-true" under a strict-accept carving — and the tilted verdict
carries "[tilted carving]" in-band. Writing this exposed a v1 gap
retroactively: v1's single THRESHOLD was itself a carving choice that
verdicts never disclosed. v1 verdicts were silently neutral-posing. The
absorption of their bias knobs forced us to make ALL carvings visible,
including the neutral one we'd been treating as default-invisible. Outside
structures audit the borrower.

## 4. Genesis proposals gained bearings

observe3's pressure keys now include the tendency string: "blurred 3 times,
leaning toward-true/toward-ignorance (knowledge-led)" — the seed data for a
new distinction now says which *direction* the old vocabulary failed in.
A future drafted responder can be direction-specific. The absorbed interior
geometry is already feeding the growth layer; corridors are composing
without being asked to.

## 5. The refusal held, and produced the cleanest contrast of the project

testify3 returns an anomaly naming the caller's impossible weight; their
library stores 1.7 and reports it as current state. Their decision_output
stays outside Lucky entirely. The register's "what was NOT absorbed" entry
is the first negative birth record — a record of vocabulary considered and
declined, with the audit as justification. Refusals need receipts as much
as adoptions; this is now precedent.

## 6. Open after v3

- Tendency-aware branch dispatch (explore3's blur:tendency keys) is
  minimally wired; weights for tendency branches are ad-hoc (half of max)
  and should be registered or derived.
- The apex axis uses their sqrt(2)/2 normalization convention, kept and
  named; whether Lucky wants a different normalization is undecided.
- Their block-wiring idea (networks of analyzers) remains unabsorbed and
  interesting: Lucky sessions do not yet compose into networks. If they do,
  the muER-laundering problem their wiring has must not be imported — the
  channel between sessions must carry full case files, not scalars.
