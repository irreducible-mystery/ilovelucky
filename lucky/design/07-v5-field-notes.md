# Field Notes: v5 (the uniform intake discipline)

## 1. A misreading as a design input

When the v5 corridor was described to Kelly ("the encoding critique
regresses onto v4 itself"), the reply read it as being about the six
uploaded files: they arrived in an arbitrary order, without stated origin,
and reading order would normally tilt the reading. That is not what the
sentence meant — the regress is inside the kernel's own doorways — but the
misreading is *true at its own level* and pointed at something the planned
v5 lacked: **arrival order is itself an intake fact.** If `paradox_suite`
(the Liar is "contained") is read before `critique_suite` (no fixed point
exists), the second file lands as a correction; in the reverse order the
first lands as naive. Same six documents, different felt verdict — unless
the reader is deliberately order-blind.

The synthesis in comparisons/05 avoided the tilt by procedure: all six
files were read completely before any assessment was written. But a
procedure that is merely followed is invisible; v5 makes it a checkable
property. Two structures in lucky5 exist because of this exchange: order
retention in the register (§2 of the module) and `order_audit()` (§3).
A wrong reading, taken seriously instead of merely corrected, produced
kernel structure. That is the dialogical loop working as designed, and it
is worth recording that the *generative* input here was an error.

## 2. The register records; it never gates

The strongest temptation in every draft was to make `take()` refuse
anonymous values. Refusal fails twice: it pushes laundering upstream (the
caller invents an origin to get past the door — an invented origin is
worse than a marked absence), and it violates the founding posture (no
escape hatches also means no *checkpoints* — the kernel points, it does
not police). So `take()` admits everything and the discipline is entirely
in the receipt. A session built on nameless intake still runs; it just
cannot *pass* as a session built on declared ground. Accountability
without authority.

## 3. Anonymous damping is the sharpest case

`iterate5()` with an origin-less damping constant that produces a
settlement returns the settlement *paired with an anomaly*: the fixed
point is real, but the choice that manufactured it is nameless. This is
the exact shape of the paradox-suite defect (hidden smoothing sold as
containment), now impossible to reproduce silently through the kernel.
Note the asymmetry: anonymous damping that yields a Cycle raises nothing —
an orbit was not *bought* by the constant, so the constant's anonymity is
ordinary rather than verdict-manufacturing. The anomaly fires only where
anonymity did work.

## 4. The tripwire is honest about being a tripwire

`order_audit()` shuffles arrival order and compares positions. Passing
proves nothing (it says so in its own result: "no order sensitivity FOUND,
not proven absent") — formal associativity proofs are unavailable in a
floating-point substrate where even addition is not exactly associative.
Failing, however, proves everything: one divergent position under shuffle
convicts the combiner. Asymmetric evidential force, stated on the result.
The demo convicts a deliberately naive "first-speaker-wins" rule in six
shuffles while the registered combiners pass.

## 5. The register indicts the kernel's own genesis

The new horizon entry is the one that bites: the register sequences what
enters a *session*, but the acts that built this kernel — which documents
were read first, which critique absorbed before which — happened outside
any session, leaving order-receipts only in prose and commit history. By
its own standard, Lucky's genesis is anonymous intake. Scene 4 of the demo
partially repairs this for the six-file episode (replaying the upload as a
registered intake story, with three of six origins statable and three
honestly nameless), but the repair is retrospective and hand-fed. A kernel
whose own construction history enters through its own register is a
corridor we can point at and not yet build.

## 6. What take() cannot ever do

Verify. An `Origin` is the world's testimony about the world; a false
origin passes as declared ground. The discipline converts silent
laundering into on-record assertion — an improvement in accountability,
not in truth. This is registered as a structural cannot, because it is
one: verification would require a second, independent channel to the
world, and anything arriving on that channel would itself need an origin.
The regress does not terminate; the register just makes every step of it
leave a receipt.
